Instructions

  1. This is a practice, not a quiz or an exam: you may use your notes and the class presentations. Nothing is saved or sent to your teacher.
  2. Use g = 9.8 m/s² (do not round it to 10). Choose a positive direction and keep it; the sign of a is part of the answer.
  3. For every problem write Given, Unknown and the absent variable on paper, choose the equation, and then type your answers.
  4. Type only the number (decimal point). The unit is shown next to each box. A numerical answer is correct within ±1.5 %.
  5. Press Check to see which answers are right. You can try again. Show solution opens the worked steps; New numbers gives you the same problem with different data.
  6. Every question shows its points and its criterion. Your practice score updates at the top.
